05

The core idea. A stock's upside above a set price moves far more per dollar than a fraction of the stock. Holders already own that upside; we let them sell it a dollar at a time, and we make buying it as simple and fun as ripping a pack.

HOW IT WORKS

Two sides, one pool, one oracle.

01 · SELLERS

Lock and list

Lock a stock, keep its base value, list the upside above the strike at your own price. Every rip of your listing pays you directly.

02 · BUYERS

Rip $1

One tap picks a position at random across 10 stocks: NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META, TSLA, SNDK, MSTR, SPCX.

03 · SETTLE

30 days later

The oracle settles each market. Above the strike, buyers redeem. Below, the rip expires and sellers keep their stock.

ERODORO RIPS

06

Sellers call lockAndList: their stock is split into a base claim, returned to them, and an upside claim, listed in the pool. Buyers call ripUp for one dollar; selection is uniform across eligible positions. The house also supplies positions with firm quotes. At the 30-day expiry each market settles on its oracle and buyers redeem. Sellers keep the downside and give up gains above the strike, like a covered call.
